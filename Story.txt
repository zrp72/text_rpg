//Syntax Key:

//For paragraphs, simply type the name of the paragraph with a : at the end. 
//Anything beyond the colon until the next empty line will be part of that paragraph.

//For options, within curly brackets, write the source and destination paragraphs seperated by a colon.
//Outside the brackets, write the prompt for the option on the same line.
//To direct to a nexus, add a ~ at the end of the destination tag.
//Example: "{Town:Store}"

//For a nexus, within square brackets, write the destination paragraph with a ~ at the end, followed by a colon outside the brackets.
//Following lines will be the destination variations followed by the attribute condition. 
//The colon between the attribute and its value is equivalent to >=
//The first variation is the default, and as such it does not need a value.
//Example:
//[Store~]:
//Bad Credit = Credit:
//Average Credit = Credit:0
//Amazing Credit = Credit:20
